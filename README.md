# Quorum ($QRM)

**Make an offer on anyone's bag. Sell yours without nuking the chart.**

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/quorum/  
**X:** https://x.com/QuorumTrade

## What you can do
- Deal desk: private deals, open offers, want-to-buy and auctions
- Offer board: take, fill, bid on or cancel deals, each settled in one transaction
- Coin list with live prices and pool swaps that show price impact
- Price-impact table: what a pool sale costs vs 0% on Quorum
- Live deal ticket, receipts, fees, $QRM fee-free tier, roadmap and FAQ
- Interactive constellation background: it reacts to your cursor, click to pulse

Single self-contained `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser.

> Playnet simulation only. Play wallet, made-up coins and pools, bots on the other side. No real funds or chains.
