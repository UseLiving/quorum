How a deal works

Both sides approve. Then it settles at once.

You sign your half

Name the token, the size and the price, or post it on the board. Your wallet signs your side of one transaction. Nothing leaves your wallet.

A small deal account keeps the signature valid for hours. Cancel any time.

They check, then sign

Only the wallet you named can take it. Their page decodes the exact deal, prices both sides live and runs the safety gate before they sign.

On the board: anyone asks or counters, you approve one, and it becomes a private deal.

It settles together

Your tokens and their SOL move in the same transaction. If either side comes up short, the whole thing fails and nothing moves.

Safety

Checked before anyone signs.

  • No custody. No escrow wallet, no program, no delegate. Your tokens stay in your wallet until the deal settles.
  • One transaction. Both sides move together, or nothing moves.
  • Warnings up front. If a coin can be frozen, minted or taxed, the deal card says so in plain words before anyone signs.
  • Can it fill? If the maker moved the bag or the counterparty is short, the page says "can't fill" before anyone signs.
  • Who is the other side? Wallet age, past deals and ties to the coin's creator, shown on every deal.
  • Cancel any time. Closing the deal voids the signature for good.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Why not just sell on the pool?

A pool walks the price down as your order fills. A deal settles at the price both sides agreed, so a big bag moves without moving the chart.

Is there a contract to trust?

No. A deal is one transaction both wallets sign. There is no escrow and nothing to drain, because nothing is ever held.

Can the counterparty change the price?

No. They can only sign the exact deal you signed, or not sign it.

Can someone else take my private deal?

No. Only the wallet you named can complete it. Open offers can be taken by anyone.

How do auctions work?

Set a floor and a timer. Bids come in; when the timer ends, the highest bid that can still pay settles.

Is this real money?

Not here. This Quorum runs on a playnet: a play wallet, simulated coins and pools, and bots on the other side. Nothing leaves your browser.
